# Articles

### All vignettes

- [European Hockey Data in R: SHL, Liiga, DEL, KHL, National League and
  More](https://fastRhockey.sportsdataverse.org/articles/european-hockey-leagues.md):

  Get schedules, standings, play-by-play and shots for 13 European
  hockey leagues in R with fastRhockey: SHL, Liiga, DEL, KHL, National
  League, ELH and more.

- [Corsi, Fenwick and PDO in R: Hockey Shot-Share Metrics with
  fastRhockey](https://fastRhockey.sportsdataverse.org/articles/hockey-advanced-metrics-corsi-fenwick.md):

  Compute Corsi, Fenwick, shot share, xGF%, shooting and save percentage
  and PDO at 5v5 for NHL teams and players from fastRhockey play-by-play
  in R.

- [Getting Started with fastRhockey: NHL, PWHL and Hockey Data in
  R](https://fastRhockey.sportsdataverse.org/articles/introduction.md):

  Install fastRhockey, load NHL and PWHL play-by-play into R, make your
  first hockey chart with ggplot2, and learn Corsi, Fenwick, xG and
  other analytics terms.

- [NHL Expected Goals (xG) in fastRhockey: How the Model
  Works](https://fastRhockey.sportsdataverse.org/articles/nhl-expected-goals-xg-model.md):

  How the fastRhockey NHL expected goals (xG) model is built: features,
  XGBoost training, evaluation, how to reproduce it in R, and its known
  limitations.

- [Hockey Shot Maps in R: NHL and PWHL Rink Plots with
  fastRhockey](https://fastRhockey.sportsdataverse.org/articles/nhl-pwhl-rink-plots-shot-maps.md):

  Draw NHL and PWHL rink plots in R: shot maps, xG-sized shot charts and
  shot heatmaps from fastRhockey play-by-play with sportyR and sdvplotR.

- [PWHL and College Hockey Data in R: Women's Pro and NCAA Hockey with
  fastRhockey](https://fastRhockey.sportsdataverse.org/articles/pwhl-and-college-hockey.md):

  Load PWHL play-by-play, box scores and schedules in R, use the live
  PWHL functions, and get NCAA men’s and women’s college hockey data
  from ESPN.

- [PWHL Expected Goals (xG): How the Coordinate Model
  Works](https://fastRhockey.sportsdataverse.org/articles/pwhl-expected-goals-xg-model.md):

  How the PWHL expected goals (xG) model behind load_pwhl_xg_pbp()
  works: shot features, logistic regression, season-holdout evaluation
  and known issues.
